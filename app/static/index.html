Find it, pick it, keep it.

Search for a preacher or a channel and load every sermon they have published. Then download one, a few, or all of them, as video or audio, in the quality you want.

Find the person

Choose Channels & people, type a name, then click Load all videos on their channel.

Narrow it down

Filter by words in the title or by length (sermons are often over 1 hour), then sort by date.

3

Download in bulk

Select all, or Shift+click to select a range. Choose MP3 or MP4 and a quality, then click Download.

Tip: you can paste a link to a playlist, a channel or a single video from almost any site in Paste a link.

4K and 1440p are only available in VP9 or AV1. "Most compatible" still downloads them when you choose those qualities.

Audio and tags

For sermons and other speech, MP3 at 96 to 128 kbps sounds clear and uses about 1 MB per minute. Use 320 kbps or FLAC for music.
