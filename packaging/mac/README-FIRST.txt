INSTALLING RIT DOWNLOADER ON A MAC

1. Drag "Rit Downloader" onto the "Applications" folder.
2. Open Applications and double-click "Rit Downloader".

If macOS says the app "can't be opened" or comes from an unidentified developer:
  - macOS 15 Sequoia and newer: open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll
    down, and click "Open Anyway" next to Rit Downloader. Then open it again.
  - macOS 14 and older: right-click (or Control-click) the app, choose Open, then click Open.
You only need to do this once.

Rit Downloader runs in the menu bar (the download-arrow icon at the top of the screen).
Click it to open the window, open your downloads folder, or quit.
The app window opens in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge if one is installed, otherwise
in your default browser.

Downloads are saved to Downloads/Rit Downloads by default. You can change this in Settings.

Optional browser extension: see the "Browser extension" folder. In Chrome, go to
chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click "Load unpacked", and choose that folder.
